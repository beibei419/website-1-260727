Introduction of our concept

Club Icarus is the club for the broken.

We are members who lost our wings, or live as fallen angels. We failed, defeated, abandoned, or lost. Yet now we assemble as a group of stunners, following the guide of the devine goddesses known as "Dall." They are virtual angels with blue blood as descendants of Artemis, coming from the unknown LOONAverse. In this club, we reborn with phoenix wings, and break traumas as borned stunners with blue blood that re-exist on human earth.





History of Club

Icarus, son of the master craftsman Daedalus, attempted to escape Crete using wings made of feathers and wax, but he flew too close to the sun, the wax melted, and he fell into the sea and drowned. After his death, his disappeared soul secretly turned to a girl with no arms borned in a small village. Her mother tells her, "Your wings will grow at the age of twenty," and dies in front of her. So the girl waited, dreaming the same dream of growing large wings every night. Yet at her twentieth birthday, her wings did not grow. Thus she went to the forest and finded a witch, asking for revenge.
It was recorded in myth that Actaeon accidentally saw goddess Artemis while she was bathing on Mount Cithaeron; for this reason he was changed by her into a stag and was killed by his own 50 hounds. His ghost turned into hunters of the flying. Meanwhile, due to the collapse of LOONAverse, Artemis turned herself into deer form and shoots it. The five scattered pieces transformed into shooting stars that performed birth on Earth, with one devine part taking over the girl.
